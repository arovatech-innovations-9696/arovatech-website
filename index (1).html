Business profile

A quick look at who ArovaTech Innovations is, what we make and how we work with you.

ArovaTech Innovations is an educational technology and robotics brand. We design and supply practical, affordable products that help students learn by building: robotic models, STEM working models, DIY kits, IoT and smart-home models, and the electronic components that go into them.

Our products are made to be understood, assembled and improved by the learner. A student who builds a robotic car or a smart-home model learns how motors, sensors, code and structure fit together, and that understanding stays with them far longer than a page in a textbook.

Beyond ready-made products, we take on custom work. Schools, teachers, makers and small businesses can bring us an idea and we turn it into a laser-cut design, a robotic chassis, a project enclosure or a working prototype.
